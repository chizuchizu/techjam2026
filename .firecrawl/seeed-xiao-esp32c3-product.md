Feature

*   **Flexible MCU Board:** Incorporate the [ESP32-C3](https://files.seeedstudio.com/wiki/Seeed-Studio-XIAO-ESP32/esp32-c3_datasheet.pdf)
     32-bit RISC-V chip, operating up to 160 MHz, mounted multiple development ports, supported by Arduino / CircuitPython
*   **Outstanding RF performance:** Implement complete Wi-Fi functions and Bluetooth Low Energy, while supporting communication over 100m with a U.FL antenna
*   **Elaborate Power Design:** Provide 4 working modes as low as 44 μA in deep sleep mode, while supporting lithium battery charge management
*   **Thumb-sized Design:** 21 x 17.8mm, Seeed Studio XIAO series classic form factor and elegant productization of single-sided components mounting, suitable for wearable devices
*   **Perfect for Production:** Breadboard-friendly & SMD design, no components on the back

**Application 1: Smart Private Garage**

**![](https://media-cdn.seeedstudio.com/media/wysiwyg/image3_3.png)**

**Materials:** XIAO ESP32-C3 \*2, [Grove - Button](https://www.seeedstudio.com/Grove-Button.html?queryID=a395b6b54a60f5b5eb4e1c7d992e91d3&objectID=2809&indexName=bazaar_retailer_products)
 \*1, [Grove - Relay](https://www.seeedstudio.com/Grove-Relay.html?queryID=6cb9802785c3b92d960e871ad057d394&objectID=1804&indexName=bazaar_retailer_products)
\*1

**Description:**

This is a peer-to-peer WiFi communication application, since two XIAOs detect each other via Wifi, the garage door will open or keep opening, and vice versa.

**Improvement:**

It is nice to add an [ultrasonic distance sensor](https://www.seeedstudio.com/Grove-Ultrasonic-Distance-Sensor.html?queryID=13d31e03bf475f170858e61c15c3b140&objectID=2281&indexName=bazaar_retailer_products)
 to measure the car-to-door distance and an [OLED](https://www.seeedstudio.com/Grove-OLED-Display-0-66-SSD1306-v1-0-p-5096.html?queryID=7c3dd343ddf7b5ead1c73c35f76c02f4&objectID=5096&indexName=bazaar_retailer_products)
 screen to check the status of the garage. If you want an onboard voice sensor to command the door to open with vocal instruction, try our [XIAO BLE Sense](https://www.seeedstudio.com/Seeed-XIAO-BLE-Sense-nRF52840-p-5253.html?queryID=c8b6534f058c3fc46fc574c96abd89d2&objectID=5253&indexName=bazaar_retailer_products)
.

**Application 2: Remote Forest Tracker**

**![](https://media-cdn.seeedstudio.com/media/wysiwyg/image4_3.png)**

**Materials:** XIAO ESP32-C3 \*1, [Grove - Wio - E5](https://www.seeedstudio.com/Grove-LoRa-E5-STM32WLE5JC-p-4867.html?queryID=cc41428ac87d37bdfbe99ab4df628551&objectID=4867&indexName=bazaar_retailer_products)
 \*1, [Grove - GPS](https://www.seeedstudio.com/Grove-GPS-Air530-p-4584.html?queryID=38e1c38ff1882395232f5ee7f8357036&objectID=4584&indexName=bazaar_retailer_products)
 \*1

**Description:**

Thanks to the tiny size of the XIAO series, it is suitable to make the wearable devices, such as a small pet trackers. This wearable device can be used as a simple GPS-Tracker which will lock its current location and publish other data via LoRa.

**Improvement:**

A [compass](https://www.seeedstudio.com/Grove-3-Axis-Digital-Compass-V2.html?queryID=fcf354905e4b63f04fa80f4e451a45ed&objectID=158&indexName=bazaar_retailer_products)
 that shows direction and distance is a great added value to the tracker that you could consider. Meanwhile, publishing [altitude](https://www.seeedstudio.com/Grove-Barometer-Sensor-BMP280.html?queryID=d8f41e3e99a48554396ba761b758cf53&objectID=496&indexName=bazaar_retailer_products)
, [speed](https://www.seeedstudio.com/Grove-3-Axis-Digital-Accelerometer-LIS3DHTR-p-4533.html)
, or other parameters could bring more scenarios to this tracker. Besides, the chip ESP32-C3 allows this tracker to communicate over WiFi or BLE, and therefore, we can upload the tracker’s data or realize the Long Range-to-MQTT bridge as well.

Probably you are worried about the wireless capability of the above application of XIAO ESP32-C3, and We have a [2.4GHz Rod Antenna for XIAO ESP32-C3](https://www.seeedstudio.com/2-4GHz-2-81dBi-Antenna-for-XIAO-ESP32C3-p-5475.html)
 which increases the wireless capability of the XIAO ESP32-C3, The antenna is certified TELEC and XIAO ESP32C3 is compatible with Antenna, Check [2.4GHz 2.81dBi Antenna for XIAO ESP32C3 - Seeed Studio](https://www.seeedstudio.com/2-4GHz-2-81dBi-Antenna-for-XIAO-ESP32C3-p-5475.html)
 to see more!
